\section{Per-State Out-of-Sample Evaluation (2026)}
\label{app:perstate}
% =====================================================================
Table~\ref{tab:perstate} reports the per-entity out-of-sample 2026
\smape\ of the four models (general stratum); its wide spread---genuine
state heterogeneity compounded by \smape\ inflation for sparse
counts---is what the per-series, revisable rule is built to absorb. It
also explains why no single model dominates the panel, rather than
indicating a shortcoming of any individual forecaster.
